  World Elite, World Elite Plus** — 0% intro rate on all; no annual fee (Platinum tier),
  $29 (World Elite), $249 + $49/additional card (World Elite Plus).

  per-bank index number). Servicing: 24-7cardaccess.com / "The Card Services Center" /
  cardaccount.net. Partner portal: portal.thecardservicescenter.com. Underwriting engine:
  cue.thecardservicescenter.com.
- Bureau: **Equifax** per mentor/community intel (Card Assets does not publish its bureau —
  treat as COMMUNITY-DP grade; confirm at application).
