### APPENDIX — EWS (Early Warning Services) Data Points

EWS (Early Warning Services — the deposit-screening bureau owned by the big banks; tracks account/transaction history and is used instead of or alongside ChexSystems) is NOT the focus of the source page. The points below are distilled from reader comments only — treat as anecdotal/lower-reliability than the ChexSystems list above. Pull your own EWS report to confirm.

Operational notes (from comments, consistent with general knowledge):
- EWS generally CANNOT be frozen the way ChexSystems can — so a Chex freeze does nothing at EWS-exclusive banks.
- Request your EWS report via secure portal with a form + ID copy; ~2 weeks, emailed. MyIDCare monitors EWS (imperfectly); it does NOT monitor Chex.
- EWS reportedly keeps roughly 1 year of inquiry history.

EWS-EXCLUSIVE (reported to use EWS and NOT pull ChexSystems — repeatedly cited):
- **Bank of America** — EWS, no Chex
- **Chase** — EWS, no Chex
- **Wells Fargo** — EWS, no Chex
- **Citizens** — primarily EWS
