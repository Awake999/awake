## H. Stanford FCU — ⭐ THE FIND (Platinum; Diamond-track after seasoning)
**Charter 13392 (VERIFIED via ncuso.org), Palo Alto, $4.8B.**

**Membership from SoCal — YES, VERIFIED, remote.** Anyone can join via **FOPAL (Friends of the Palo Alto Library)** or **MOAH (Museum of American Heritage)**; per Doctor of Credit and maximizingmoney.com, **any CA resident joins free — Stanford FCU pays the nonprofit dues**. Online join at sfcu.org/join; identity verification offered via **Virtual Branch Zoom call**, so a Fontana/Long Beach owner never needs to visit Palo Alto. (Membership share $5, normally paid by SFCU.) Remote *business* account opening is INFERRED (Virtual Branch advertises discussing business banking) but not page-verified — sfcu.org returns 403 to scrapers, so all sfcu.org citations below are via 2026-06-10 search snapshots of sfcu.org pages.

**Products — both card and LOC, self-issued (VERIFIED):**
- **Business Rewards Visa** — credit limit **up to $50,000**. 1pt/$1; 2pts dining/food-delivery. **No annual fee, no balance-transfer fee, no cash-advance fee, no foreign-transaction fee.** APR 16.00–17.99% variable (Prime-based). Disclosure hosted on sfcu.org itself ("Business Visa Application and Solicitation Disclosure") — self-underwritten, no Elan/TCM/FNBO anywhere.
- **Business Line of Credit** — **up to $100,000 unsecured**; above that requires collateral. Plus equipment/term loans and SBA 504.

**Gates (VERIFIED, quoted from sfcu.org):** minimum **640 FICO**, **two years in business**, and **Stanford FCU membership for a minimum of six months** before the business card. → **Action: open the $5 membership now to start the 6-month clock**, even before needing credit.

**Docs:** start with a **Business Loan Application + Personal Financial Statement** (VERIFIED snippet). Tax returns not mentioned for the card (UNKNOWN; the $100K LOC likely fuller doc). Not a no-doc shop, but PFS-based card underwriting at $50K is light for the limit.

**DPs:** myFICO #6036159 — personal Visa approved **$7,500** asking $10K at 672 EX / 693 TU / 693 EQ, 42% util, $72K income (COMMUNITY-DP; thread fetch blocked, date unconfirmed). No public business-card DPs found — thin DP base is the main unknown.

**Chex/EWS:** UNKNOWN. DoC checking-bonus pages note pull type unconfirmed.

**Verdict: Platinum.** Best self-issued $50K business card + $100K unsecured LOC that a SoCal business can actually reach. The 6-month seasoning + 2-yr TIB + full-app process (no instant online approval) keep it below Diamond.
