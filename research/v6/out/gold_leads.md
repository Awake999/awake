## Ranked leads

### 1. U.S. Bank — Cash Flow Manager LOC (ALREADY_IN_V5 — gold program intel)
The single best Enterprise-B&T-class find, and it's verifiable on the bank's own site: for Cash Flow Manager lines **under $50K, "financial statements are not normally required"**; only above $50K do they ask for the last fiscal-year tax return. - VERIFIED 2026-06-10: https://www.usbank.com/business-banking/business-lending/business-lines-of-credit/cash-flow-manager.html and https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0209629.html
- Action: apply at the $49K line size to stay in the no-financials tier.

### 2. American Express Business Line of Credit (Business Blueprint, ex-Kabbage) (issuer ALREADY_IN_V5; product likely missed)
$2K–$250K line where underwriting is a **real-time bank-account link — no tax returns or financial statements** for most line sizes (>$150K reserved for existing Amex relationships). 660+ FICO, 12 mo TIB, ~$3K/mo revenue. Online, national, fast.
- VERIFIED 2026-06-10: https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/business/blueprint/business-line-of-credit/ (+ help-center FAQ "What's required to apply").

### 3. Ramp corporate charge card (NEW_LEAD)
EIN-only, **no personal guarantee, no personal credit pull**. Underwrites the linked business bank balance (~$25K minimum cash; limit typically 10–20% of balance, so $50K+ limits are routine with ~$250K+ on deposit). Registered entity required (no sole props). National online.
- VERIFIED vendor terms + community comparisons (2026): https://www.glencoyne.com/guides/corporate-cards-us-startups , https://corporatecreditcardforstartups.com/ramp-vs-brex-vs-divvy-vs-amex/
- Honest framing: it's a charge card whose limit scales with real cash — lenient, not magic.

## Scam / integrity flags
1. **"Rize CU $50K card" headlines** circulating in high-limit listicles: that is a **secured** card requiring a matching $50K deposit. Not funding; ignore.
2. **Paid "funding plug" courses** (the $2K–$5K coaching tier around YouTube business-credit influencers) overwhelmingly resell three public strategies found above for free: Chase Ink stacking, US Bank's <$50K no-financials tier, and Amex bank-link products. Any pitch withholding the institution name until payment = no independent DP = treat as UNVERIFIED-RUMOR.
3. **"EIN-only, no-PG $50K revolving cards for new LLCs"** claims: the only legitimate no-PG products found (Ramp/Brex/Divvy, Sam's Club type-2) all require either real cash balances, real revenue, or an established business credit file. Anything else implies misstating revenue on applications — do not pursue; that is bank fraud exposure, not underwriting leniency.
4. Several myFICO/Reddit DPs are 2024–2025; Synchrony and FNBO tighten in credit downturns. Re-pull DPs within 60 days of application sprints.
