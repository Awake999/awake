Business Spending account

Six months after you open a Stanford FCU business checking (spending) account, you can apply for the Business Rewards credit card with a minimum 640 credit score and two years in business.

Business Spending account

The Business Rewards credit card delivers a solid ROI on everyday purchases 

Your typical business day probably includes everything from office supplies to lunch with clients. Make sure some of that cash flows back to you by using our Business Rewards card. Every dollar you spend on food and food delivery earns 2% cash back, and all other purchases earn 1% cash back, which quickly builds up as statement credits every month. The more you spend, the more you get back. 

  • No annual fee means no hassle 
  • Get a cash advance or transfer your balance from another credit card to save money with a lower rate—all fee-free
  • Use this Visa credit card for purchases around the world without a foreign transaction fee
  • Go digital by adding your card to your mobile wallet for more security and convenience 
  • Set up alerts to keep an eye on transactions and activity
  • Lock your card when you don't need it, and unlock it easily when you do 
  • Tell us about your upcoming travel plans so we make sure your transactions process smoothly 
  • Get the financial flexibility your business needs with a credit limit up to $50,000 

Start the process by filling out a Business Loan Application and Personal Financial Statement.
