## Candidates

### 1. American Plus Bank, N.A. (dba International City Bank) — TARGET
Bank, CERT 58469, $891M. 249 E Ocean Blvd, Long Beach — **0.56 mi from 1 World Trade Ctr**. Open to the public (bank; LB branch = ex-United Fidelity/ICB, converted May 2025). Business charge card exists: **Visa Charge Company Rewards** — but the card program is **TCM Bank, N.A.** (agent program): bankaplus.com/Credit-Cards sends "view and compare all cards" to **mycommunitycc.com**, TCM's application platform (TCM servicing = cardaccount.net). So the card credit decision is TCM's, not APB's. In-house: commercial loans, small business loans, CRE, construction. No published limits; no biz LOC explicitly listed. Apply: branch 562-436-9800 or via mycommunitycc.com. Chex/EWS, bureaus: UNKNOWN. Confidence: Medium-High.

### 2. Rize Credit Union (ex-SCE FCU) — TARGET
CU, charter 7835, $1.31B. Ontario branch 7.88 mi from Fontana 92336 (OSM still tagged "SCE FCU" — rebrand confirmed). **Joinable from both anchor addresses** via free American Consumer Council membership (also SEGs incl. Southern California Edison; only ID/UT/LA/TX residents excluded). **Business Visa Platinum: published $5,000–$50,000 limits**, no annual fee, employee cards with custom limits. No Elan/TCM/cardaccount tells — appears self-issued/serviced (own digital banking). Rates/terms and docs not published — "speak with a loan consultant" / business inquiry form. Confidence: Medium-High.

### 3. Southland Credit Union — TARGET (Long Beach only)
CU, charter 68415, $1.29B. 1050 Linden Ave — 1.14 mi. FOM: live/work/worship/school in **LA or Orange County** → joinable from 90802, **not** from Fontana (San Bernardino Co.). Business checking (two tiers), business account application PDF (sole prop/LLC/corp/nonprofit/HOA), commercial loans (equipment/inventory/expansion). Cards are **self-issued** ("issued by Southland Credit Union pursuant to a license from Visa USA Inc."); published consumer cap **$25,000 max total unsecured**. A dedicated *business* CC/LOC could not be verified online (site 403-blocks bots) — phone-confirm. **ChexSystems confirmed** on all new accounts. Confidence: Medium.

### 4. Alta Vista Credit Union — REFERENCE / secondary target
CU, charter 64531, $212M. Rialto branch 4.91 mi from Fontana. Community charter: live/work/worship in **San Bernardino County** (+ Riverside Co. residents/employees, SEGs) → **joinable from Fontana 92336**. Has Small Business Checking + Commercial Lending + small-business digital services, but **no business credit card surfaced**. Ex-Norton AFB CU. Worth a call only if a Fontana-county LOC matters. ### 5. Thinkwise Credit Union — REFERENCE (conditional target)
CU, charter 7770, $121M. Rialto branch 3.91 mi from Fontana. **Has both business credit cards and a business LOC**, with the clearest doc list in this cohort (govt ID for signers/25%+ beneficial owners, EIN/SSN, FinCEN BO cert, formation docs, business license/Home Occupation Permit, $25 deposit). But FOM-gated: opener must already be a member (school employees, SB/Orange/Riverside counties) **or** the business must be HQ'd in those counties **and demonstrably support the educational sector**. Address alone does not qualify. Apply: mybusiness@thinkwisecu.org / 909-474-8448. Restricted: cannabis, gambling, MSB, adult. Confidence: Medium-High.

### 6. Navy Federal Credit Union — REFERENCE (affiliation-gated)
CU, charter 5536, $203.6B — biggest CU in the country. Ontario branch 7.18 mi from Fontana. **Business cards real: GO BIZ Rewards (Visa or Mastercard)**, plus business checking/loans. Self-issued. The honest gate: membership requires **military affiliation** (active duty/retiree/veteran, immediate family/household member, or DoD civilian), and **all business owners must be NFCU members**; reviewers cite ~1 yr individual membership or ~2 yrs in business and ~750 score; application requires wet signature (no e-sign), submitted via business credentials. Bureau DPs: community-reported **TransUnion and/or Equifax** pulls (well-documented in DoC/myFICO threads, not officially confirmed). Like CAHP (highway patrol), this is employer/affiliation-gated — actionable only if a principal has the affiliation. Confidence: High on gate/products.
