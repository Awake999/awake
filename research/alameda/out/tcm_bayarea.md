## TCM new-business underwriting profile (TIB/seasoning/doc/bureau)
- **App-only** approval on the personal guarantor's credit (small business / startup friendly).
- **No tax returns** required for requests **under ~$25K**.
- **No time-in-business minimum** (no seasoning requirement) — usable by brand-new entities.
- **Credit bureau: Experian (EX)** — TCM is widely reported to pull Experian for new applications and
  for credit-limit increases (myFICO community reports). Treat the single-bureau pull as Experian for
  CA-state applicants pending a hard cite from TCM directly.
- Cites: https://www.tcmbank.com/community-bank-solutions/credit-card-program/business-agent-program ;
  https://ficoforums.myfico.com/t5/Credit-Cards/TCM-Bank-credit-cards-any-thoughts/td-p/6303273
