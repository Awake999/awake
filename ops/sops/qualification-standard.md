# Qualification Standard — Main Offer vs Downsell vs Unqualified
*(Mirror of Notion SOP `3cc5bb1ffef481aa8c8ce7c33bade43b` — Notion is the editable original. For Kai + all setters/closers.)*

## 1 — QUALIFIED, MAIN OFFER 🟢
Strong credit profile (3–5+ cards, one $10K+ limit, 2+ yr avg age, no heavy derogs) **AND** money to invest in the program. Both, together.

## 2 — DOWNSELL, CREDIT REBUILD 🟡
Fixable credit problems: late payments, charge-offs, collections, hard inquiries, thin file. That IS the downsell offer — if they can invest at the ~$200-deposit level.
**Never work: bankruptcies, child support.**

## 3 — UNQUALIFIED 🔴
- **3a No funds** — cannot cover even the ~$200 entry. Count this cohort; its size measures ad-targeting quality.
- **3b Offer mismatch** (Constantine: market/offer incompatibility = unqualified regardless of profile). Archetypes: Jennifer U. (wanted $500K no-personal-liability funding; we require PG), Ghani A. (licensed brokers only; we are consulting + database + DFY).
- **3c Shoppers / no real need.**

## Metrics ladder (report separately, never blended)
1. Raw show rate · 2. Qualified show rate · 3. Close rate on qualified-main-offer sat calls · 4. Downsell-cohort outcomes · 5. PRIME path: speed-to-lead hit + human triage → show & close of that cohort.
