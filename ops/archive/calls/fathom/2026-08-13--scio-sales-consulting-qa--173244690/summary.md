## Topics

### Handling Non-Decision-Makers (NDMs)

  - [**Problem:** Pitches to NDMs (e.g., marketing staff) fail because the DM rarely joins the follow-up.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=308.0)
      - [**Data:** 90% no-show rate for DM calls; \~30% close rate on the 10% who show.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=624.0)
      - [**Result:** A highly inefficient 3% close rate (3 deals per 100 calls).](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=652.0)
  - [**Solution: Pivot to a 2-Step Process**](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=467.0)
    1.  [**Discovery Call (NDM Only):** Build rapport and gather info. Do not pitch.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=467.0)
    2.  [**Presentation Call (DM Required):** Frame the follow-up as presenting a custom plan that requires the DM to answer their specific questions.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=467.0)
  - [**If the NDM acts as a gatekeeper:**](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=794.0)
      - [**Ask for the DM's contact info** to schedule directly.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=794.0)
      - [**If they refuse, question their seriousness** to create leverage.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=860.0)
      - [**Book the NDM call anyway** but use post-call logistics to secure the DM's presence.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=941.0)

### Managing Client Sales Performance

  - [**Problem:** Clients blame marketing for low sales, creating churn risk and weak case studies.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=999.0)
  - [**Solution: Set Expectations in Discovery**](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=1584.0)
      - [**Frame the ROI with realistic ad-based close rates** (e.g., 20–30%) before pitching.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=1627.0)
      - [**Get client confirmation** that they are confident closing at this rate.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=1235.0)
      - [**Explain the volume game:** Success requires a large sample size (e.g., 100 calls for 20–30 closes) over a longer horizon than a few weeks.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=1382.0)
  - [**Solution: Integrate Sales Coaching**](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=3170.0)
      - [**Use existing meetings** (e.g., bi-weekly) for both marketing and sales review.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=3170.0)
      - [**Require clients to record calls** (e.g., using Fathom) and submit links for analysis.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=3223.0)
      - [**Provide actionable feedback** on recordings to improve their closing skills.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=1353.0)

### Handling Pre-Call Price Pushback

  - [**Problem:** Prospects demand pricing before a discovery call, risking the sale.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=2102.0)
  - [**Solution: A 3-Stage Escalation**](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=2163.0)
    1.  [**Explain Value:** State that price depends on their specific needs, which you can't know without discovery.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=2397.0)
    2.  [**Offer a Hot Transfer:** If they persist, offer to share your screen and walk them through the process *now*.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=2255.0)
    3.  [**Pitch on the Spot:** If they still refuse, give a 2-minute overview and the price. This is a last resort for cost-focused prospects.](https://fathom.video/calls/782299252?tab=summary&timestamp=2333.0)
