### Problem: Ineffective Dispute Tracking

### Solution: Attorney General Escalation Strategy
- Rationale: an ex-bureau employee confirmed AG involvement forces a legal response.
- Process: (1) build a comprehensive paper trail of all dispute attempts; (2) file a complaint with the client's state AG (KY for Ashwini, CA for Matthew); (3) the AG's office disputes directly with the bureau.
- Why it works: bureaus face daily fines (up to $25k) for non-compliance when the AG is involved.
