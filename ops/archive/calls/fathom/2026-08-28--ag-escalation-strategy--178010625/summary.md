### Problem: Stalled Disputes & Inefficient Workflow

### Solution: Attorney General Escalation Strategy
- Rationale: an AG complaint forces the bureau's legal team to intervene, bypassing standard support and increasing removal success.
- Process: (1) build a paper trail of all dispute actions (letters, calls, CFPB complaints) with dates and times; (2) file the complaint with the client's state AG (e.g., Kentucky for Ashwini); (3) attach the Google Doc evidence ledger and a CFPB report.
- CFPB report download SOP: log in to the CFPB portal, click the complaint ID, then click the "Print Complaint" button to expand all sections before saving as PDF. Do NOT use the browser's native print (Ctrl+P) — it omits details.
