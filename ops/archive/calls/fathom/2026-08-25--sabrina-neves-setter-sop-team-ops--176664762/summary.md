### SOP: Handling Unqualified Leads

  - [**Goal:** Assess if a lead can afford a custom solution, even if they don't qualify for the main offer.](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=2244.0)
  - [**Process:**](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=2588.0)
    1.  [**Disqualify:** State the lead doesn't qualify for the main offer.](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=2614.0)
    2.  **Pre-frame:** Offer a custom solution as a favor.
          - [**Script:** "Given you don't qualify for our main offer, if we could create a custom solution to resolve \[problem\], do you think this would be a good fit for you?"](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=2622.0)
    3.  **Assess Cash Flow:** Ask about their financial situation.
          - [**Script:** "What's your cash flow situation looking like? Are you W-2 employed or do you have a business bringing in profit?"](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=3286.0)
          - [**Follow-up:** "At the end of the month, what's your typical take-home/net income after all expenses?"](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=3438.0)
    4.  **Book Appointment:** If viable, book a call with a funding specialist.
          - [**Prerequisite:** Lead must provide credit reports (Experian, Credit Karma) or GHL logins.](https://fathom.video/calls/798393367?tab=summary&timestamp=4305.0)
