  - [**Problem:** Current Slack-based EODs lack real-time visibility and are difficult to search for performance tracking.](https://fathom.video/calls/801601613?tab=summary&timestamp=36.0)
  - [**Solution:** A new Trello "Accountability Board" will serve as the central hub.](https://fathom.video/calls/801601613?tab=summary&timestamp=36.0)
      - [**Process:** Employees will live-track tasks with timestamps on a daily Trello card.](https://fathom.video/calls/801601613?tab=summary&timestamp=126.0)
      - [**EODs:** The Trello card will prompt for a structured EOD (completed, incomplete, time lost), which is then copied to Slack.](https://fathom.video/calls/801601613?tab=summary&timestamp=1149.0)
