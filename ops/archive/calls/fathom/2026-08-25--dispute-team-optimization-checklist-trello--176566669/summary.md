### Solution: The "Optimization Checklist"

  - [This checklist defines the specific state a client's credit profile must reach before being considered complete and ready for funding.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=939.0)
  - [**Personal Information Optimization:**](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1033.0)
      - [**Name:** One name, spelled *exactly* as on the DL/SSN card (no aliases, matching middle names/initials).](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1069.0)
      - [**Address:** As close to one as possible (ideally 1–3 max), matching the DL/bills.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1244.0)
      - [**Phone Numbers:** Zero.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1299.0)
      - [**Employers:** Zero.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1330.0)
      - [**Personal Statement:** Zero.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1414.0)
  - [**Negative Remarks:**](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1645.0)
      - [Zero late payments, charge-offs, collections, bankruptcies, repossessions, or negative child support marks.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=958.0)
      - [Zero hard inquiries.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=923.0)
      - [Search for and remove "Unrated" marks (a low-priority item that can signal bankruptcy to underwriters).](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=1673.0)

      - [**Why:** Recent client profiles (Todd, Ed, Ashwini) were found to have unauthorized fraud alerts and security freezes, which block funding applications.](https://fathom.video/calls/798180477?tab=summary&timestamp=2529.0)
