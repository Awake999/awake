Role Overview
We are looking for a detail-oriented and proactive *Disputes & Funding Manager* who will initially focus on reviewing disputes and optimizing client profiles for underwriting approval, with the opportunity to transition into a *Funding Manager / Account Manager* role.

• Proactively identify potential underwriting objections and address them before submission.
• Track disputes, documentation, submissions, approvals, and outstanding requirements.

• Maintain detailed and accurate records in the CRM.
*Funding & Account Management*
• Transition into managing clients through the funding process.
• Serve as the primary point of contact for assigned clients.
• Collect and manage required documentation and stipulations.
• Coordinate with underwriting, sales, and operations to move files through the funding process.
• Provide clients with clear updates and next steps.
• Monitor applications and proactively follow up to prevent delays.
• Manage ongoing client relationships after funding.
• Identify opportunities for repeat funding and account growth.
• Ensure a high level of client service and retention.
Requirements
• Strong attention to detail and organization.
• Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
• Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
• Ability to manage multiple client files simultaneously.
• Comfortable working with financial information and documentation.
• Strong follow-up and time-management skills.
• Ability to work independently and take ownership of assigned files.
• Experience in lending, underwriting, credit, financial services, disputes, funding, or account management is preferred.
Ideal Candidate
The ideal candidate is *analytical, persistent, organized, proactive, and highly communicative*. They can identify problems, find solutions, and confidently manage clients through a process.
Growth Path
*Disputes Specialist → Funding Specialist → Funding Manager / Account Manager*
