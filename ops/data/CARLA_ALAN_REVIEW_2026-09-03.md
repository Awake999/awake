## 🟩 THE UNIFIED PLAN — one design that meets every requirement on both sides

**Principle:** stop asking Carla to be three things. Give her **one primary role with a written scope**, **one growth track with booked hours and a measurable exit**, and **one honest comp conversation** — all on the calendar, none "on your own time."

### 1. Name the role in writing (this week, in the Wed 2 PM 1:1)
- **Primary (paid base): Operations Lead.** Scope = the things Alan actually needs off his plate and Carla has already shown she does well: meeting structure and agendas, Trello hygiene and the weekly plan, hiring campaign execution, tooling/access, Submagic/creative ops, LinkedIn. **Not** SOPs unless assigned; **not** dispute management; **not** lead-cycle tracking.
- **Growth track (commission on top during ramp): Closer-in-training.** Explicit, dated, with an exit test (below). Alan's counsel says "no base for closers" — that applies to a *closer*, not to an ops lead spending ~25% of paid time in a training track with a decision date.
- Write both on one page. Carla drafts it from this doc; Alan edits; both sign in Notion. Ends the "did I assign that?" loop.

### 2. Move the hours on-clock (fixes the only real fight)
- **Block 2 hours/day, 4 days/week, inside 7 AM–5 PM PT** for closer training: the program modules, Alan's sales call recordings, Constantine sessions. **Active, with notes**, the way she learns. That is 8 h/week → the 40–60 h Alan estimates in **5–7 weeks**. The Cash Cabin week counts double.
- Ops work absorbs the other ~6 hours. If ops can't fit, that is the signal Alan needs to hire the ops manager Carla already proposed ([Sep 3 32:09](https://fathom.video/calls/809142730?timestamp=1929)).
- Alan's "audio in the background" stays as a bonus, never as the plan.

### 3. Build the curriculum once, so it stops living in Alan's head
- Carla's ask is the right one: **strategy-by-profile cheat sheet** — "if the profile looks like X, the play is Y." Alan speaks it in a 90-minute recorded session (the way he did the 605B flow with Lynn [Sep 3 1:35:27](https://fathom.video/calls/809142730?timestamp=5727)); Carla and Claude turn it into the document. Alan's cost: 90 minutes, once.
- **Program walkthrough:** Carla watches the full client program before any sales call — she has never seen it ([39:33](https://fathom.video/calls/809142730?timestamp=2373)).
- **Loom on every build** Alan does alone (forms, survey, automations) — he agreed; make it a rule.

### 4. Exit test for the closer track (so "ready?" stops being a feeling)
- Shadow 5 live closes → roleplay 3 profiles against Alan → run 3 live calls with Alan silent → **decision at week 6:** (a) move to closer comp, or (b) stay Ops Lead and hire a closer. Either answer is fine. No answer is not.

### 5. The comp conversation — plainly, Wednesday
- Alan says what he told Lynn, in Carla's own review: current output vs current pay isn't working, he wants her, he sees her in sales, here is the path and the date. Carla says what she told Lynn and the call: base security matters, she wants sales, she wants ops influence, she needs the hours on-clock. The one-page role doc is the resolution.
